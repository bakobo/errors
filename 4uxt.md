# ARG_CAP is 80 and the number was never argued; raise it to a stated flood guard and say what it is not for. Approved by Daniel 2026-08-25 (heti Q-7DTM). The docstring's REASON is sound -- values arrive from the wire, a message may be rendered or logged, so no arg is echoed unbounded (error-handling.md rubrics 2 and 7) -- but both rubrics say 'capped' and neither says 80, so the number came from whoever lifted the module. What the tight cap costs: it clips messages the application wrote itself. heti's multisig-rotate refusal is 165 characters of its own prose with no interpolation at all, cut at 'with a coun...', so the reader is told there is no way to say something with a count and never learns the remedy is a weights list with 0 for the leaver. Sixteen such sites in heti (heti tick 7rba). What makes the tight cap unnecessary for screens: heti already bounds every error arg downstream in register.reword via tui.safe, at FIELD_CAP=200, a number whose docstring ARGUES it -- two and a half rows of an eighty-column terminal, short enough that no field can scroll an authoritative line. So on a heti screen there are two bounds, the reasoned one is looser, and the arbitrary one upstream means it never bites. The clip also happens at CONSTRUCTION (BakoboError.__init__ stores capped args), so logs lose the tail too. Fix: raise to a flood guard in the hundreds or a thousand, and rewrite the docstring to say it is a flood guard and not a legibility rule, pointing consumers at their own display bound for that. Do NOT go to 255: it fixes heti's longest offender by luck and makes an attacker-chosen 400-char value three times worse on any consumer without its own bound.
